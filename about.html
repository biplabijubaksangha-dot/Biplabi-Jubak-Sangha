Biplabi Jubak Sangha

Biplabi Jubak Sangha is a registered youth organization working for social welfare, cultural development, sports, education, environmental awareness, and village development.

Our members actively organize Ganesh Puja, blood donation camps, tree plantation drives, sports competitions, temple development, awareness campaigns, and various community service activities.

We believe that unity, teamwork, and active participation of young people can create positive and meaningful change in our community.

About Salijanga

Salijanga is a village located in Naugaon Block/Tahasil of Jagatsinghpur district, Odisha. The village comes under Sikhar Gram Panchayat and Sikhar Revenue Circle, with Bodal as its Post Office and PIN Code 754113.

Salijanga represents the enduring spirit of rural Odisha, where community life, agriculture, faith, culture, tradition, and youth participation come together to shape the identity of the village.

Location

Village Profile

According to Census 2011 records, Salijanga had a population of 1,023 people living in 257 households.

The village covers approximately 111 hectares and has a predominantly rural and agricultural character.

Agriculture

Agriculture is an important part of the economic and social character of Salijanga.

Census land-use records show approximately 93.78 hectares of net sown area, including approximately 70.82 hectares of irrigated land.

Education

Education plays an important role in the development of the village and its younger generation.

Census-based village records indicate the presence of a government primary-school facility, while students also have access to educational institutions in nearby areas.

Spiritual & Cultural Heritage

Salijanga's Spiritual Identity

Salijanga has a distinctive spiritual heritage associated with the Mahima tradition. Salijanga Matha is documented as an acclaimed centre of the Mahima cult and was established by Baba Kulamani Das.

The Sunya Mandira is identified as an important attraction of the Matha. The site is associated with ideals of social equity, fraternity, and spiritual values.

The village is also home to Baba Sidhweswar Temple, an important religious place connected with the traditional spiritual life of the local community.

Salijanga Mahima Ashram

A documented centre of the Mahima tradition, associated with Baba Kulamani Das and the Sunya Mandira.
