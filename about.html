About Us

Together for Community Development & Social Service

Biplabi Jubak Sangha

Biplabi Jubak Sangha is a registered youth organization working for social welfare, cultural development, sports, education, environmental awareness, and village development.

Our members actively organize Ganesh Puja, blood donation camps, tree plantation drives, sports competitions, temple development, awareness campaigns, and various community service activities.

We believe that unity, teamwork, and active participation of young people can create positive and meaningful change in our community.

Mission

To inspire youth through unity, discipline, social service, and leadership.

Vision

To build a stronger, cleaner, educated, and united society.

Our Values

Unity, Respect, Service, Honesty, Teamwork, and Leadership.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring young people and the local community together for positive social change.

We work to promote unity, education, cultural values, environmental awareness, sports, and community welfare.

Through voluntary participation and teamwork, we aim to support those in need and contribute to the overall development of our village.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a united, responsible, educated, and progressive community where every young person has the opportunity to participate and contribute.

We aspire to create a cleaner, healthier, safer, and more developed village while preserving our cultural heritage and strengthening community spirit.

Our Values

Our values are based on unity, honesty, service, respect, responsibility, discipline, and teamwork.

We believe that working together with sincerity and dedication can create meaningful change.

We respect our traditions, care for our environment, support our community, and encourage the younger generation to become responsible citizens.

Our Journey

Since its establishment, Biplabi Jubak Sangha has continuously served the village through religious events, youth development, sports tournaments, environmental initiatives, and various welfare activities.

The organization believes in working together for the betterment of society and encouraging young people to participate in nation-building and community service.
