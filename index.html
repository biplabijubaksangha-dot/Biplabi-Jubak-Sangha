Biplabi Jubak Sangha

Biplabi Jubak Sangha is a registered youth organization dedicated to social service, education, sports, cultural development, environmental awareness, and village welfare.

Our members work together to organize Ganesh Puja, blood donation camps, tree plantation drives, sports events, temple development, and various community welfare programmes.
