Ganesh Puja

Ganesh Puja Celebration

Ganesh Puja is one of the most celebrated cultural and religious events organized by Biplabi Jubak Sangha.

Every year, members of the Sangha come together with great devotion, enthusiasm, and unity to celebrate Lord Ganesha.

The celebration includes puja rituals, cultural programmes, community participation, and various social activities.

It brings people together, strengthens brotherhood, and encourages the younger generation to preserve our cultural traditions and values.

The programme also provides an opportunity for children and young people to participate in cultural activities and learn the importance of tradition, discipline and community cooperation.

Blood Donation

Blood Donation Camp

Biplabi Jubak Sangha organizes blood donation camps to encourage voluntary blood donation and support patients who are in need of blood.

Blood is one of the most valuable gifts that a person can give to another human being. A single blood donation can help save lives during medical emergencies, accidents, surgeries, and other critical situations.

Our members, volunteers, and local residents come together to participate in these camps and spread awareness about the importance of voluntary blood donation.

We encourage eligible and willing donors to come forward and contribute to this noble cause while following proper medical guidance and safety procedures.

Through these initiatives, Biplabi Jubak Sangha aims to promote humanity, social responsibility, and the spirit of helping others in our community.

Donate Blood • Save Lives • Serve Humanity

Tree Plantation

Tree Plantation Drive

Biplabi Jubak Sangha organizes tree plantation drives to promote environmental awareness and create a greener and healthier village.

Members of the Sangha and local residents come together to plant trees in suitable public and community areas. These activities encourage people to understand the importance of trees and environmental protection.

Trees help improve the environment, provide shade, support biodiversity, and contribute to a cleaner and healthier future.

We also encourage young people to take responsibility for protecting nature and caring for planted trees.

Plant Trees • Protect Nature • Build a Greener Future

Sports Activities

Sports & Games

Biplabi Jubak Sangha organizes sports competitions and recreational activities to encourage young people to stay active, healthy and disciplined.

Sports provide an excellent opportunity for young people to develop teamwork, leadership, confidence and a spirit of healthy competition.

Our sports programmes encourage participation from local youth and help create friendship, cooperation and unity among community members.

We believe that physical fitness and positive participation in sports play an important role in the overall development of young people.

Play Together • Stay Healthy • Grow Together

Education

Education & Awareness

Biplabi Jubak Sangha believes that education is one of the most important foundations for the development of young people and society.

We encourage children and young people to continue their education, develop knowledge and build awareness about important social issues.

Through community activities and awareness programmes, we encourage the younger generation to understand the importance of education, discipline and responsible citizenship.

Our goal is to motivate young people to learn, develop their skills and contribute positively to their families and community.

Educate • Inspire • Empower

Social Service

Community Social Service

Social service is an important part of the work of Biplabi Jubak Sangha. Our members come together to support villagers and contribute to community welfare.

We organize and participate in various activities that address community needs and encourage people to help one another.

Our volunteers believe that even small efforts can make a meaningful difference when people work together with unity and dedication.

Through voluntary service, we aim to strengthen cooperation, compassion and social responsibility within our community.

Serve Society • Help Others • Make a Difference

Temple Development

Temple & Community Development

Biplabi Jubak Sangha supports temple development and community religious activities that help bring people together.

Members contribute their time, effort and cooperation towards maintaining and improving community religious spaces.

Religious and cultural programmes also provide an opportunity for people of different generations to come together and participate in community activities.

Through collective participation, we aim to preserve cultural traditions while promoting unity, cooperation and respect within the community.

Tradition • Unity • Community

Clean Village

Clean & Healthy Village

Biplabi Jubak Sangha promotes village cleanliness and environmental awareness through community participation and voluntary activities.

Our members encourage residents to keep roads, public spaces and community areas clean and to avoid improper disposal of waste.

Clean surroundings are essential for a healthy community. We believe that maintaining cleanliness is a shared responsibility.

By involving young people and local residents, we aim to create awareness and develop a habit of keeping our village clean and beautiful.

Clean Village • Healthy Community • Better Future

Youth Development

Youth Development & Leadership

Biplabi Jubak Sangha is committed to encouraging young people to become responsible, confident and active members of society.

Our activities provide opportunities for youth to participate in community programmes, develop teamwork and learn the importance of discipline and cooperation.

We encourage young people to take initiative, develop leadership skills and use their abilities for the benefit of the community.

Through sports, cultural programmes, social service and community activities, young people can learn valuable skills while contributing to society.

Our vision is to create a generation of responsible and socially conscious youth who can contribute positively to the development of the village and society.

Youth Power • Leadership • Responsibility
